Yuzu for iPhone

The focus app that's never mad at you.

Mochi is a capybara in a hot spring. Put your phone face down and his water stays warm. Pick it up early and nothing bad happens; the water just cools a little, and he waits.

How it works

Start a soak. Choose 10, 25 or 50 minutes and put the phone face down. The screen stays on, dimmed, so picking it up shows Mochi, not the lock screen.

Checking on him is free. Locking the phone, unlocking it, or lifting it to look does not end the soak. Switching to another app for more than a few seconds does.

Warmth and yuzu. Every finished soak warms the water and earns yuzu, the fruit you trade in the bathhouse shop for hats, floats and new onsens. The first soak of each day earns double. Nothing is ever taken away.

Common questions

Does the water go cold forever? No. It cools slowly while you're away and warms back up with the next soak. Mochi dozes when it's cold; he never shivers and never leaves.

What does Yuzu Plus include? Double yuzu on every soak, two more onsens (the seaside bath and the rooftop sento) and four Plus decorations (harvest moon, cherry branch, Winter Solstice, floating tray). Soaking, the snow and bamboo onsens, the rest of the shop and Mochi himself are free forever.

How do I cancel or manage a subscription? Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancelling keeps Plus active until the end of the paid period.

I bought Plus and the app doesn't show it. Open the paywall from the shop and tap Restore purchases. If it still doesn't show, write to us with the email on your Apple receipt and we'll sort it out.

Does the app collect my data? No. Everything lives on your phone. See the privacy policy.

The soak ended when I picked up the phone. Make sure you're on version 1.0 or later; earlier test builds counted an unlock as leaving. If it still happens, tell us which iPhone and iOS version.
