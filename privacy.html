Privacy policy

Yuzu does not collect, store or share personal data. This page says exactly what the app touches and why.

What Yuzu stores, and where

Your pet's state (warmth, yuzu balance, items, the name you gave him, soak history) is saved only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. We don't run accounts, so there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to recover from a server.

Purchases

Yuzu Plus subscriptions and yuzu packs are sold through Apple's App Store and processed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app receives from Apple only whether a purchase is active. We never see your name, email, card or address. Receipts and refunds are handled by Apple.

Analytics, advertising and third parties

Yuzu contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. We don't use identifiers for advertising and don't request the tracking permission. Apple may share aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics with us (downloads, crashes if you opted in to share them with developers); those contain nothing that identifies you.

Children

Yuzu is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Your rights (EU/EEA and elsewhere)

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or erase. If you believe we do hold something, write to us and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority; in Spain that is the AEPD.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date, and the app's App Store page links to it.
